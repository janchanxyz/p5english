# Example grammar fix report (P5)

- **Total checked:** 627
- **Source mix:** mostly hand-authored P5 sentences; overlapping P4 examples reused where still correct; a few POS templates rewritten
- **Focus:** articles (a/an, including *an honest*), countable nouns, British/HK spelling (*colourful*, *organise*, *recognise*), natural collocations, subject–verb agreement, adjective vs adverb use

## What was fixed

- Replaced awkward template leftovers (“…is important in our daily life”, “I learned a new fact about…”, “She felt honest after the good news”)
- Corrected sport collocations (play basketball/baseball, not kick)
- Added articles before countable food nouns (*a dumpling*, *a biscuit*, *a pancake*, *a sausage*)
- Normalised a/an before vowel sounds (*An honest friend…*)
- Kept British/HK spelling throughout examples

All 627 entries have both `example` and `exampleZh` (zh-HK Traditional Chinese).
